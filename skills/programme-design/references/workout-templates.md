# Workout templates from Nippard's own training

Paraphrased from his 2025 videos on the routine behind his measured one-year transformation (Jan 2024 – Mar 2025: about +2.7 lb lean mass on DEXA after 15 years of training). Use these as proven starting templates, adjusted to the user's profile.

His split that year mixed upper/lower (strength-leaning) with push/pull/legs (hypertrophy-leaning) across the week. [Experiment](https://rosetta.to/u/jeffnippard/how-much-muscle-did-i-gain-in-365-days-scientific-experiment).

## Upper body (one year)

| # | Exercise | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Incline barbell press | ~3–4 × 6–10. Slightly narrow grip, touch-pause on chest, safety pins for failure sets. His #1 pec builder |
| 2 | Seated cable fly | ~3 × 10–12. Drive the elbows together, keep them high, sweep an arc |
| 3 | Weighted pull-up | ~3 × 6–8. Chest to bar, elbows down and in, pause at the bottom. He progressed +30 → +60 lb for 6 reps over the year |
| 4 | High cable lateral raise | Sweep out, not up; no shrugging |
| 5 | Deficit Pendlay row | Torso near parallel, fast up, slow down, straps if grip limits; lengthened partials after full-ROM failure |
| 6 | Cable overhead triceps extension | Set the cable high, step under and forward to get into position |
| 7 | Biceps, rotated | Bayesian cable curl on upper day; preacher + hammer curls on pull day |

Approach: last set of each exercise to failure; steady load progression; compounds first, isolation after. [Summary](https://boxlifemagazine.com/nippard-1-year-transformation/), [exercise list](https://generationiron.com/jeff-nippard-transformation-workout/).

## Legs (one year)

| # | Exercise | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lying leg curl | Hips pressed into the pad, grip handles; partials at the end of the last set |
| 2 | Pendulum squat (or hack/Smith/barbell) | 3 × 6–8; early sets 1–2 RIR, last set to failure; full depth (film it) |
| 3 | Romanian deadlift | Heavy, ~6 reps; hips back, bar close, stop around the knee |
| 4 | Leg extension | 1–2 hard sets; seat all the way back, hold the handles, 3-s lowering if knees complain |
| 5 | Hip abduction machine | Sit tall (hips extended), close to failure, add load over time |
| 6 | Straight-leg calf raise | 8–10; deep stretch at the bottom, top half optional; last set ends with a ~30 s hold in the stretch. Put calves first if they get skipped |

He deloaded every couple of months. [Source](https://rosetta.to/u/jeffnippard/the-leg-workout-i-followed-for-my-1-year-transformation).

## Shoulder specialisation (Dec 2025)

From *How I Blew Up My Shoulders*. [Source](https://rosetta.to/u/jeffnippard/how-i-blew-up-my-shoulders).

1. **Frequency:** train delts 3–4× per week with about 6–8 hard sets per session; they recover fast.
2. **One heavy vertical press**, about 5–6 reps, progressed linearly all year (deload every 4–8 weeks).
3. **Isolation by double progression** (e.g. 10–12 reps), switch exercises when progress stalls.
4. **Cue:** "scoop sand" out to the side; keep traps quiet.
5. **Both lengths:** add crossbody cable laterals or Y-raises for the long position, but dumbbells work too.
6. **Fill the gaps:** incline Y-raises (front/side), 45° cable rear-delt pull (rear/side).
7. **Get leaner:** he rates lower body fat as possibly the biggest visual change for 3D delts.

## Warm-up pyramid for the first heavy lift

About 50% × 5 → 70% × 3 → 85% × 1–2, then working sets. Add light cardio first. See [[training-science]].
