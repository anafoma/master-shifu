# Exercise tiers

Nippard's best-and-worst rankings (2023–2026), paraphrased. Criteria: stretch under tension, feels good, simple progression (see [[principles]]). Tiers rank **muscle-building potential only**, not strength or athletic value.

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| S | His favourites; use if you can |
| A | Strongly recommended |
| B | Useful in some contexts; better options may exist |
| C | Better options exist for most people |
| D | Probably not a good choice for most people |
| F | Generally not recommended |

Pattern across every list: stable machines and cables that load the stretch win; combo moves and exercises with no tension at the bottom lose.

## Upper body

| Muscle | #1 | Other S | Strong A options | Bottom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chest | Machine chest press | Seated cable fly | Barbell and dumbbell bench (flat, incline), Smith bench, dips, pec deck, cable and dumbbell flyes | F: hex press, plate press, dumbbell pullover (better for lats), guillotine press |
| Back | Chest-supported row | Lat pulldowns (wide, neutral, single-arm), Meadows row, cable rows; **pull-ups moved up to S (he says he updated this in 2025; mentioned in his Jan 2026 25-exercises video)** | Single-arm DB row, Kroc row, deficit Pendlay row, lat pullovers | F: renegade row, row-plus-curl combos, above-knee rack pulls. Deadlift only C for back |
| Side delts | Cable lateral raise (cable at hand height) | Cable Y-raise, behind-the-back cuffed lateral raise | Lean-in DB lateral raise, side-lying raise, machine lateral raise | C: banded lateral raise |
| Front delts | Machine shoulder press | — | Seated DB press | D: front raises (his worst shoulder exercise) |
| Rear delts | Reverse cable crossover | Reverse pec deck (sideways for more stretch) | Rope face pull | B: bent-over DB reverse fly |
| Biceps | Face-away Bayesian cable curl | DB and machine preacher curl, preacher hammer curl | EZ-bar curl, incline curl, hammer curl, cable curl | F: waiter curl. C: spider, drag, Scott curl, 21s |
| Triceps | Overhead cable extension (straight bar) | Barbell skull crusher | Pressdown, single-arm overhead extension, cable kickback, close-grip bench, Smith JM press (S in his Feb 2026 "strangest exercises" video) | Lowest: DB kickback (nothing D/F) |

Research behind two picks: overhead extensions grew triceps about 40% more than pressdowns (2023 study he cites), and preacher curls beat incline curls head-to-head.

## Lower body

| Muscle | #1 | Other top picks | Bottom | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quads | Hack squat | S: high-bar back squat, pendulum squat (his current favourite squat), Smith squat, Bulgarian split squat; leg extension near-perfect (seat back to bias rectus femoris). A: front squat, low-bar squat, 45° leg press, reverse Nordic | F−: Bosu squat. F: jump squats, squat-to-press combos | His tier list |
| Glutes | Walking lunge (long stride + forward lean for glutes) | S: machine hip abduction (15–20 reps), 45° back extension, Smith lunge front foot raised. A: machine hip thrust / glute drive, squats, Bulgarian split squat, step-ups, RDL, cable kickback | D: donkey kicks, fire hydrants, kettlebell swing. **Barbell hip thrust only B** (a fine substitute for a glute-drive machine) | His tier list |
| Hamstrings | Seated leg curl | RDL (less fatiguing than conventional deadlift for similar glute/ham stimulus), 45° back extension | Lying leg curl works but grew less in research | [Maeo 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33009197/) + his picks |
| Calves | Standing calf raise, straight knee | Lengthened partials (bottom half) | Seated raises grow the gastrocnemius less | [Kassiano 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37015016/) |
| Abs | Cable crunch (easy to overload weekly) | Weighted crunches, leg raises | — | His 25-exercises list |

Hamstrings: over 12 weeks seated curls grew the hamstrings 14% vs 9% for lying curls; the extra came from the muscles crossing the hip, more stretched when seated. Calves: training only the stretched half grew the gastrocnemius more than full or top-half reps.

## Nippard's "only 25 exercises you need" (Jan 2026)

A full-physique shortlist, in his ranked order where known: squat (#1), pull-up, incline bench, RDL, chest-supported T-bar row, cable lateral raise, preacher curl, overhead cable triceps extension, leg extension, seated leg curl, bench press, walking lunge, overhead press, deadlift, Bayesian curl, glute drive, weighted dips, reverse pec deck, pec deck, cable crunch, neck curls/extensions, wrist curls/extensions, standing calf raise, DB shrugs, machine lat pullover. Use it as a default menu when equipment is normal and the user has no strong preferences. [Source](https://rosetta.to/u/jeffnippard/the-only-25-exercises-you-ever-need).

## Technique notes from 2026 videos

- **Incline angle:** EMG testing found upper chest activation was similar from about 15° to 45°; very steep angles did worse. Pick the angle that feels best and progress it.
- **Shoulder press:** side delt activation was high, sometimes higher than front delt. Presses count partly toward side delts.

## Using tiers well

- Tiers are expert opinion. "Feels good" is personal: a B-tier exercise you can push hard beats an S-tier one that hurts.
- Write-ups sometimes disagree by a tier (e.g. upright rows appear as D in one source, B in another). Don't lean on fine distinctions.
